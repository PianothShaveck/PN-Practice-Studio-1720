# PN Practice Studio

An English-language, static study companion for the Canadian REx-PN. Original AI-authored practice content, created October 1, 2026.

## Included

- 1,720 original scenarios (120 revised legacy items plus 1,600 newly authored items) across all eight Client Needs areas; single-choice and multiple-response items, including text/table exhibits.
- Five separate calculation templates: weight-based acetaminophen volume, divided daily amoxicillin doses, hours/minutes pump rates, gravity drip rates and remaining total fluid allowance. Parameter variants do not increase the authored-scenario count. Stated medication safety checks and relevant order context distinguish arithmetic from authorization to administer.
- Daily practice (8, 12, 20, 30 or 50 questions), focused sets, a calculation lab and a fixed 90-question timed session with a four-hour deadline.
- Explanations for every option, saved questions, notes, error reasons and simple spaced review.
- After-answer Google Search links and copyable critical-review prompts, also available in the completed-session summary. These are manual external checks; no API, automatic validation or imported verdict is involved.
- Local progress, JSON export/import, province selection and date-based 2022/2027 plan selection.
- Responsive phone/desktop layout, keyboard-accessible controls and optional WebMCP progress/navigation tools.

## Run

Serve `dist/` from any static web host. There are no runtime dependencies, API calls, paid AI services, account requirements or server-side data storage. Clicking Search Google sends that question, options and study context to Google in a new tab. Copy review prompt copies the question plus the proposed answer and rationales for manual use elsewhere; neither action includes your notes or your selected answer. Google decides whether to show an AI Overview, and its result can be incorrect. No external result changes the app’s score. Browsers without clipboard access show a selectable text dialog. Fonts are an optional external Google Fonts request; system fonts are the fallback.

For direct local opening or GitHub Pages use the portable distribution generated by `python make-portable.py`: its single `index.html` embeds all app code, styles and question data and can be opened without a local server. Google Fonts are omitted in that portable version, making it self-contained.

## GitHub Pages

1. Create a repository, e.g. `pn-practice-studio`, on the desired GitHub account.
2. Upload the portable package's `index.html` and `.nojekyll` to the repository root.
3. In Settings → Pages, choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch **main**, folder **/ (root)**, then Save.

Alternatively, use this source repository: in a Pages workflow publish the `dist` directory, or place all its files at the Pages root. All asset links are relative and work under a repository subpath.

No secret or API key belongs in this application. A future live AI generation feature would require a server-side service and independent content review; it is deliberately not presented as an existing feature.

## Study content and limitations

The 2022 and 2027 official REx-PN plans have the same eight midpoint weights: 21, 13, 9, 11, 9, 17, 11 and 9 percent; category ranges are ±3 percentage points. The 2027 plan takes effect January 1, 2027 and updates activity statements, including the safety category name. Date selection changes the label and relevant plan context, not the finite authored bank. The bank has not been audited against every activity statement.

CNO's REx-PN FAQ specifies that provincial health systems, history, policies and laws are not examined by REx-PN; Ontario jurisprudence is assessed separately. General professional judgement, consent, confidentiality and safe collaboration remain relevant. Provincial resources are supplementary context, not a claim that province-specific legal trivia belongs to the REx-PN blueprint. See https://www.cno.org/become-a-nurse/examinations/registered-practical-nurse-rpn-registration-examinations/rex-pn-frequently-asked-questions.

Daily selection uses only unseen authored scenarios, balanced by category deficits. When the bank is exhausted, Daily stops; Review and focused practice remain available. Focused and timed sessions prioritize unseen scenarios but can repeat prior cases. Arithmetic templates appear only in Calculation lab. Category-filtered or nearly exhausted sets may be shorter than requested. At 30 new cases daily, 1,720 cases cover 57 full sessions plus 10 questions; this is a volume calculation, not a guarantee of complete curriculum coverage.

Learning partial credit for multiple-response items is `max(0, selected correct - selected incorrect) / number correct`. Exact accuracy requires all and only the correct options. This is not an official scaled REx-PN score. The fixed-length timed session does not reproduce CAT, the official pretest-item design or graphic interaction. It makes no pass prediction.

**Independent AI review is not external clinician, regulatory or psychometric validation.** Per-ID review ledgers and source-check limitations accompany the review export. Revised items must match the reviewed content hash; a review of an earlier draft is not automatically approval of a later version. This remains supplementary educational practice, not a complete preparation course or clinical decision tool. References include general further reading and targeted checks; their presence alone does not signify item validation. Provincial standards take precedence over US textbook material for Canadian professional rules. Ontario RPN and BC LPN are the intended practical-nursing contexts; BC RPN denotes psychiatric nursing.

Questions and explanations are original; no official sample questions, recalled exam items or paid question banks are reproduced. No affiliation or endorsement by NCSBN, CNO or BCCNM is implied. REx-PN is a mark of NCSBN.

## Progress and privacy

All learning records stay in this browser's localStorage. Different browsers, origins, devices and local files may have separate storage. There is no automatic cross-device synchronization. Export JSON before changing host, clearing browser data or switching devices, then import on the destination. Imports replace history, preferences, bookmarks, exclusions and notes; active sessions and frozen daily snapshots are deliberately not imported. Historical answers, notes and stable IDs are preserved when the bank updates. On a bank-version change, unanswered snapshots are refreshed so revised content is used; already submitted responses in an active session are retained with the question version actually answered. Daily snapshots older than seven days are pruned while answer history remains. Failed storage access is reported visibly. There is no telemetry or server-side question submission. Hosting providers may process ordinary web access logs.

## Verification

`node tests.mjs` checks bank consistency, answer/rationale alignment after shuffling, deterministic daily sets, unseen-first selection, review intervals, filters, timed-session size, 500 generated arithmetic variants, blueprint boundaries and backup validation.

A separate DOM integration harness verified all six views, daily completion, duplicate submission prevention, calculator-question feedback, deferred timed-session scoring, escaped notes and progress serialization. Optional WebMCP contracts were exercised using a simulated registry; no supported browser WebMCP context was available. A visual real-browser/device test was not available in the authoring environment. Qualified clinical review remains outstanding.

## Content and external review workflow

Final authoring and review artifacts are stored under `content/`. `node scripts/build-bank.mjs` integrates the four authored groups, preserved-ID legacy patches and review metadata. `node scripts/export-bank.mjs /absolute/output/path` generates a complete TXT and JSON bank, blocks of 50 with and without keys, an ID manifest, representative calculation cases and the external-review prompt. Blind blocks omit titles, learning objectives, explanations and reference cues. No learner data is included.

`node bank-tests.mjs` verifies the agreed 1,720-question volume, reference integrity, exact question/option uniqueness, 58 sequential daily sets without repetition, exhaustion behaviour, new session sizes and bounded snapshot retention. `scripts/audit-similarity.py` provides lexical pair triage; it requires scikit-learn for development and cannot prove semantic originality or educational quality.
